# Computational Studios

The computational studios are interactive simulations that accompany the lecture notes. Each one appears as a numbered figure in its section and also opens in its own browser tab. They run entirely in the browser, so nothing needs to be installed. The table lists them in the order they appear in the course.

| Studio | Section | What you can explore |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Real Gas](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/real-gas-studio.html) | [1.4](chapter-01/section-04.md) | The ideal-gas law, the van der Waals equation, and the Peng–Robinson equation for five gases, compared through the compressibility factor $Z$ and the isotherms, with liquid–vapor coexistence below the critical temperature. |
| [Two-State System](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/two-level-system.html) | [2.2](chapter-02/section-02.md) | How the canonical probabilities, partition function, and heat capacity respond to the energy gap and the temperature range. |
| [Ideal Gas](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/ideal-gas-studio.html) | [2.5](chapter-02/section-05.md) | Atoms of helium, neon, or argon in a cube, with $\Lambda$, $q_{\mathrm{trans}}$, $U$, $P$, and $S$ from the partition function as $N$, $V$, and $T$ change. |
| [Harmonic Oscillator](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/harmonic-oscillator-studio.html) | [2.6](chapter-02/section-06.md) | The probabilities of the vibrational levels, the internal energy, and the heat capacity of a harmonic oscillator, for eight diatomic molecules or any vibrational temperature. |
| [Linear Rigid Rotor](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/rigid-rotor-studio.html) | [2.7](chapter-02/section-07.md) | A diatomic molecule turning about its center of mass, with the rotational partition function, the probabilities and energies of the levels, and the rotational entropy. |
| [Phase Diagram](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/phase-diagram-schematic.html) | [6.1](chapter-06/section-01.md) | The $P$–$T$ phase diagram of water, with the molecules and the densities of the phases at a state that you choose. |
| [Clausius–Clapeyron](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/clausius-clapeyron-studio.html) | [6.2](chapter-06/section-02.md) | Clausius–Clapeyron fits of the vapor pressures of water, ethanol, and benzene over a range of temperatures that you choose, with the residuals and $\Delta\bar h_{\mathrm{vap}}$ from the slope beside the reference value. |
| [Chemical Equilibrium](https://wexlergroup.github.io/chem-4020-5020/computational-studios/equilibrium-visualization.html) | [7.1](chapter-07/section-01.md) | Equilibrium of $2\,\mathrm{NO_2} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{N_2O_4}$ as the minimum of the Gibbs free energy, the mixture at any extent of reaction, and how temperature and pressure shift the minimum. |
