The cube is one unit cell, with an edge of 5.63 Å. It contains four molecules. The 14 molecules shown are those at its corners and on its faces, which it shares with the neighboring cells. Each molecule lies along one of the four body diagonals of the cube. A flip exchanges the carbon atom and the oxygen atom of one molecule. The atom positions are those of the X-ray structure of L. Vegard, Z. Physik 61, 185 (1930).
