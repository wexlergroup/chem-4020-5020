Energy fluctuations and system size

Independent two-state particles exchange energy with a heat bath. Change the number of particles, and compare how far the energy of the system strays from its average.

Energies that the system has visited (bars) and the binomial distribution (orange). The tallest value of the distribution sets the height.

Each particle has two states, with E1 = 0 and E2 = ε = 10 meV, and the particles do not interact. In each step, a tenth of the particles, chosen at random, exchange energy with the heat bath: each of them is then in state 2 with the probability p2 = e−ε/kBT/(1 + e−ε/kBT). The steps are not a physical time.
