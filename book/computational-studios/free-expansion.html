Number of particles N

The particles do not interact, as in an ideal gas. nL is the number of particles in the left half, and Ω = N!/[nL! (N − nL)!] is the number of ways to choose which particles they are. The entropy is S = kB ln Ω. The dashed lines mark the even split and the largest possible value of ln Ω.
