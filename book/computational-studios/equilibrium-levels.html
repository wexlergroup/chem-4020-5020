Energies are in units of an arbitrary energy ε, and all levels are measured from the lowest level of A. The levels of A lie 2ε apart, and those of B lie 0.5ε apart. The length of a bar is the population of its level relative to the lowest level of A, which is the Boltzmann factor e−E/kBT (dashed curve). qA and qB are the sums of the bars of each form over all levels, including those above the range drawn.
